How I work

Design that's accountable to the business

I work across the line between product management, design, build and commercial reality, close to the problem and close to the people who pay for the result.

01

Product & systems thinking

I work on projects on multiple levels, from gathering requirements to mapping information architecture, so the product holds up under real conditions.

02

I build what I design

Self-taught in HTML, CSS, and AI-assisted development, I turn ideas into testable prototypes in hours rather than sprints, enabling validation with real users before committing engineering time.

03

Connected to outcomes

Moving closer to the commercial side sharpened my approach: I design with outcomes in mind and measure impact. Even the best solution has to work economically to be worth shipping.

04

Close to the field

I work with installers, operators and field teams, because software for complex field environments only succeeds when it's shaped by the realities it runs in - such as lack of internet connection on site.
